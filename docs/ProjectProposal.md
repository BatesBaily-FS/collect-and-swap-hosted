# Collect and Swap Book Sharing App

## Application Definition Statement

_A clear high-level summary should be used to introduce the concept. This should be similar to an elevator pitch or a conversational reply to "What are you building?”. You will explore the audience and their demographics, the problem they are having, and your solution in subsequent sections in more detail but touch on them here._

_Clarity over quantity should be the focus, to that end, creating visuals/charts to explain the more complex data or logical points could help to reinforce your Application’s Definition Statement._

I am building a web-based mobile application for trading and selling books. This app would allow users to create an account to display their book collection online and view other's collections. Users could make requests to initiate a book trade or buy books from others' collections using account points or tokens. These in-app currencies can be purchased or acquired through account activity. Users will have access to create or join book clubs to discuss and connect with fellow fans. If a desired book can't be found through another user, the locator page will allow them to search using geolocation to find local bookstores and libraries. Lastly, users can post local events such as book meet-and-greets, reading groups, bookstore opening, etc on the events page. The purpose of this app is to encourage users to repurpose or share unused books, creating a sense of community among like-minded people and minimize unnecessary spending.

## Problem Statement

_In a few sentences explain the problem your target market is seeing that requires this project to be built. This will identify why is your application needed and needs to be supported by Primary Research._

Many avid readers struggle to affordable ways to acquire physical books and often have unused books they wish to share. Traditional purchasing methods can become a high expense for readers that consume a lot of material, and there is a lack of community-focused platforms for book sharing and discussion. This application addresses the need for convenient, cost-effective way to trade and sell books while fostering a sense of community.

## Pain Points

_Explain your audience’s pain points that are contributing to their defined problem and their impact on the user. Primary Research should support your explanations._

- High Costs: Reader feel that continuously purchasing new physical books is increasingly expensive, especially for students and young adults.
- Underused Books: Many readers have books sitting on shelves that they no longer read and would like to pass on to others.
- Lack of Community: Readers often feel isolated and seek to make connections with others who share passion for books.

## Solution Statement

_How is your project going to solve the problem outlined above? Consider the competing products in your market space. What makes your solution different from other’s attempts to solve the problem? How are you able to better solve the defined problem for your audience than your competition?_

The Collect and Swap Book Sharing App provides a platform for users to trade and sell books affordability. Unlike existing platforms and apps that focus solely on sales, this app encourages community engagement through user generated local events like book clubs or meet-and-greets. By allowing user to earn points through activity and offering geolocation features for find local resources, this app will create a unique, interactive experience that promotes book sharing and community involvement.

## Competition

_What competing products exist to solve this or a similar problem? Identify and summarize competing products and how their approach to solving your identified problem differ from your own._

- BookMooch: A book trading site that allows users to swap books but lacks a community engagement aspect.
- GoodReads: Platform primarily for book tracking and book reviews without trading or selling functionalities.

## Features & Functionality

_Define key features and functionality intended to provide solutions to specific problems and pain points you have identified. These key items should be specifically defined in response to problems / pain points._

_A good way to identify a Key Feature is to use the phrase 'In order to [solve this problem] I need to [do this]'. For example, 'In order to listen to music while I workout I need to be able to create a playlist'._

_Features and functionality should be prioritized based on their importance to the user. This will help you focus on the most important features first and then add additional features as time allows._

- Trade Requests: Users can initiate book trades. This feature allows them to swap books affordably, addressing the pain point of high book prices and enabling users to grow their book collect without the significant financial burden.

- Geolocation Locator: This feature helps users find nearby bookstores and libraries. By integrating a geolocation tool, the app enhances its utility, allowing users to easily discover local resources for acquiring books, solving the issue of accessible and costly reading material.

- Book Clubs: Users can create or join clubs to discuss favorite books. This fosters community interaction and connection among book lovers, addressing the desire for social engagement and greater community focused around literature.

- Event Posting: Users can share local book-related events within the app. This feature promotes engagement and connection by allowing users to participate in community activities, solving the challenge of finding and attending literacy events within your area.
