## Sub-Topic 1 - Significance of color choices

Brief overview of research.

- Color is a key component of your business's branding, and also helps communicates messages to users as they interact with the site. Be mindful of color psychology and the effect colors can have on peoples emotions of you site visitors (WP Engine, 2024).

- This consideration goes much further than picking colors you like, with the help of color theory you can strategic pick a color that aligns with the aesthetic and overall goal of your app. The primary color is the first to consider, for my case I am thinking green. Color theory suggests green is reminiscent of growth, nature, environment, and money and I feel like these ideals align with my app which focuses on community connection and repurposing resources. Next to consider is secondary colors, a good number to have is 3. These colors should compliment the primary color and work in harmony including the colors of backgrounds and text.

- Now that I have established my primary color, its important to understand how the different shades of green affect the viewer emotions and perception. Lighter shades of green lean that closer to yellow tend to give emotions of freshness and excitement. Darker shades of green are more associated with stability and growth. I personally feel like the perfect shade for my app leans closer to darker side, as I feel it more closely aligns with the purpose of my app. I know one of the secondary colors will be light gray or white as it complements the primary color and also will help brighten up the pallet more appealing to the younger target audience (Wharton, 2019).

## Sub-Topic 2 - Branding Strategies

Brief overview of research.

- When defining brand identity it is important to clearly outline the purpose of your brand and what you envision for its future. This includes identifying the principles that guide your brand's actions and decisions that will resonate with the target audience. Identify what makes your product unique and utilize this to differentiate your brand from competitors.

- Its important to understand your audience by conducting market research to better understand your customer's preferences and behaviors. Focus on engaging with your audience through meaningful interactions and feedback to build loyalty.

- Create a consistent brand experience by developing a strong visual identity (logo, color pallet, and typography) and establishing a brand voice that communicates your message consistently across all platforms. Ensure that customer interaction enhances their experience with your brand, both online and offline.

## Sub-Topic 3 - Design trends

Brief overview of research.

- Minimalism and simplicity are popular design trends, emphasizing clean lines, open spaces, and a focus on essentials elements. This approach enhances the usability and helps users easily navigate content while creating a visually appealing aesthetic.

- Bold Colors and Gradients are increasingly being used in design to create dynamic and eye-catching visuals. Designers are incorporating bold, saturated colors that evoke emotion and grab attention. Gradients in particular are being applied creatively in backgrounds, buttons, and illustrations to add depth and visual interest.

- The incorporation of 3D designs and augmented reality features are gaining traction, providing users with interactive and immersive experiences. AR is also being utilized in applications to enhance customer experiences, such as virtual try-ons and interactive displays. This innovation not only enhances the users experience but also drives engagement and conversation about your brand, making it a vital trend in modern design.
