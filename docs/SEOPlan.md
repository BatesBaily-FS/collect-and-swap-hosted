# SEO Plan for Collect & Swap

---

## Target Audience

Our target audience includes:

- **Avid readers, students, and community members**
- Individuals passionate about accessing, trading, and sharing books affordably.
- Users engaged in book clubs, library visits, and active on book communities (Reddit, GoodReads, TikTok, Instagram).

---

## Methodology of Selecting Keywords & Metadata

**Research Process**

- Analyze audiences interests, habits, and online behavior from marketing plan.
- Popular keyword research tools: Google Keyword Planner, Mangools, and AnswerThePublic.
- Identify frequently searched terms and assessed competition.
- Reviewed competitors SEO strategies
- Prioritized long-tailed keywords based on user intent (ex, "swap books near me", "book trading app")

## Proper Use of Meta Tags

- Meta tags (title, description) are added in the HTML `<head>` of every main page.
- Meta tags are **not displayed on the page itself** but show up in the search results and social previews.
- Each page should have:
  - A unique `<title>` tag using primary keywords
  - A matching `<meta name="description">` describing the page including keywords.
- Optimized meta tags improve click-through rates and visibility on Google, Bing, and other search engines.

---

## Using SEO to Reach Our Audience

- Selected keywords match our audience's common search terms - making our site discoverable for queries like "trade books online" and "book swap near me".
- Long-tailed and local keywords connect us to engaged, relevant users.
- High-quality meta data and content boost visibility across all search engines.

---

## Content Updated

- Integrate chosen keywords naturally into sites content (homepage, headings, subheading, paragraphs).
- Ensure every page has an individual, keyword-rich meta title and description.
- Regularly review analytics and keyword tools to update site content and SEO strategy as trends evolve.
